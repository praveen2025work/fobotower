What the dates depend on

Raise these now (long lead)

  • UAT and Prod platform requests (Prod needed by 11 Dec)
  • Network access to MB Rec, MOTIF, FAS and the LLM gateway
  • Model and data approval for the LLM gateway
  • DPIA, AI risk assessment and penetration test slot
  • MB Rec extra data and end-of-day trigger; FAS test access
  • Diagnostics hosting outside AWS/BCP

Assumptions

  • Team: 2 backend, 1 DevOps, 1 QA, 1 BA, PM and architect; frontend part-time; Product Control SME part-time
  • Product Control available for set-up in S1–S2 and UAT in S4–S5
  • Change freeze 11 Dec – 4 Jan: Prod built before it, released on 5 Jan
  • Users are already provisioned and testing their skill in finance agent chat

Top risks

  • Prod not ready by 11 Dec: platform and network requests are the critical path
  • The build deployment is failing today; fix in progress
  • Posting adjustments to MOTIF through FAS needs controls sign-off and FAS access in UAT
  • Late approvals (model, DPIA, penetration test, CAB): each week late moves go-live by about a week
